## 2. Detailed Store Description

**Peak Xender** is the all-in-one cold email outreach console engineered for sales professionals, agency owners, and e-commerce growth teams who demand high inbox deliverability at scale.

Connect your Gmail accounts securely via Google OAuth 2.0 or custom SMTP mailboxes, upload prospect lead lists with automated headerless CSV parsing, craft high-converting AI-powered spintax copy, and dispatch multi-step drip sequences with intelligent throttling.

### 🚀 Key Features

* **🛡️ Deliverability & Reputation Shield**:
  * Real-time **SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and MX** record diagnostics with instant deliverability health scores.
  * Anti-bot pre-fetch filtering to stop enterprise security scanners (Barracuda, Proofpoint, Mimecast) from skewing your campaign analytics.

* **📬 Multi-Sender Mailbox Rotation**:
  * Pool multiple Gmail and SMTP accounts into a single campaign.
  * Automatically distributes send volume across active accounts to protect domain reputation and respect daily provider limits.

* **⚡ Global Command Palette (`Ctrl + K`)**:
  * Lightning-fast keyboard-first navigation to search campaigns, prospect dossiers, inboxes, templates, and delivery logs in milliseconds.

* **🔥 Hot Lead Sentiment & Webhook Alerts**:
  * AI-powered two-way inbox sentiment classification (`Hot Lead`, `Interested`, `Question`, `Unsubscribe`).
  * Instant in-app alerts and webhook dispatches to Slack or your CRM the second a qualified buyer replies.

* **✨ AI Copywriter & Dynamic Spintax Engine**:
  * Generate high-converting cold email sequences with DeepSeek, GPT-4o, Claude, or Gemini.
  * Deep variable personalization with `{first_name}`, `{store_name}`, `{company_name}`, and `{niche}` tags.

* **📊 Real-Time Audit Logs & CSV Export**:
  * Trace granular delivery receipts, server response codes, and export filtered campaign logs with a single click.

## 3. Chrome Web Store Permission Justifications

| Permission / Scope | Why It Is Required |
| :--- | :--- |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send` | Used strictly to send outreach campaigns scheduled and triggered directly by the user from their own authorized Gmail mailbox. Peak Xender does **not** read private personal emails or access Google Drive. |
| `storage` | Stores local UI preferences, recent search history, and device viewing state securely. |
| `notifications` | Alerts users when campaigns finish or when a prospect replies with high buying intent (`Hot Lead`). |
